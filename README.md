# Roxana User Guide

Roxana is a tool for structured rational discussion. Each discussion is divided into two columns: **Propositions** (claims) and **Arguments** (logical connections between propositions). Participants read, write, and vote on sentences together.

---

## Getting started

Visit [tryroxana.com](https://tryroxana.com) and sign in. You will land on the discussions list, which shows all public discussions and any private discussions you belong to.

To start a new discussion, click **new** under Public or Private discussions.

---

## The discussions list

Each row shows:
- The discussion **ID** (a short code, e.g. `x4yu`)
- How long ago it was last updated (e.g. *3 hours ago*)
- A summary of the discussion's goals

Private discussions show the names of all participants in brackets before the summary.

You must be signed in to create a discussion. Anyone can read public discussions without signing in.

---

## Inside a discussion

A discussion has two sections side by side:

- **Propositions** — numbered statements (1, 2, 3…). These are the claims under discussion.
- **Arguments** — lettered lines (A, B, C…). Each argument is a sequence of proposition numbers that together form a logical chain.

On mobile the two sections stack vertically.

---

## Writing

Click into any empty line to start typing.

- In **Propositions**: type a plain statement. Example: *Socrates is a man.*
- In **Arguments**: type a space-separated sequence of proposition numbers. Example: *1 2 3*

Press **Enter** to save. Press **Shift+Enter** to save and move to the next line. Press **Escape** to discard your changes.

A sentence you are editing appears with a yellow background. If another participant is currently editing a sentence, you will see *"… username editing"* on that line.

Argument inputs are validated: only existing proposition numbers are accepted, duplicates are rejected, and a red underline indicates invalid input.

Click **new** at the bottom of either list to add a new sentence.

---

## Voting on sentences

Each sentence has an annotation area on the left. Click it to cast or change your vote:

| Action | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Accept** | You agree with this sentence |
| **Reject** | You disagree |
| **Clear** | Withdraw your previous vote |

Your vote is shown as an icon next to the sentence. Icons from all participants are visible at a glance.

---

## The sentence modal

Click the **number or letter** to the left of a sentence to open its detail panel. The modal shows:

- Every participant's vote (accepted / rejected / cleared) with their name
- Who has marked this sentence as a **goal**
- Buttons to **hide/unhide** the sentence
- A **set goal / clear goal** button (propositions only)

Close the modal by clicking the number/letter again.

---

## Goals

Marking a proposition as a goal signals that it is what you are ultimately trying to establish in the discussion. A solid blue border means you have set it as your goal; a dashed blue border means other participants have.

The goals summary is shown in the discussions list to give a quick overview of what each discussion is about.

---

## Hiding sentences

Any participant can hide a sentence. Hidden sentences are faded out. If any sentences are hidden, a **show hidden** toggle appears at the top of the discussion — switch it on to reveal them.

---

## Viewing argument composition

Arguments reference propositions by number. Click the **→** icon on the right side of an argument to highlight the propositions that argument references. Click again to clear the highlight.

---

## Discussant filters

A row of switches at the top of the discussion shows each participant's username. Toggle a switch off to hide all sentences from that person. This is local to your view and does not affect other participants.

---

## Private discussions

Private discussions are invite-only.

- **Create invite link**: generates a shareable URL.
- **Copy invite link**: copies it to your clipboard.
- **Revoke invite link**: invalidates the link so no new participants can join via it.

When someone visits an invite link they are added to the discussion automatically.

---

## The sync indicator

A small dot appears in the top-right area of the navbar:

- **Green** — all your changes are saved.
- **Red** — changes are pending or a sync error occurred.

If the indicator stays red, try refreshing the page.
